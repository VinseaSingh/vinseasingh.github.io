I am a PhD Candidate (thesis submitted, awaiting defense) at <a href='https://www.nbrc.ac.in/newweb/'>National Brain Research Centre (NBRC)</a>, India. My research focuses on understanding how ongoing brain states (or prestimulus activity) shape conscious multisensory perception. In my doctoral work, I have identified EEG prestimulus signatures (like periodic and non-periodic spectral components, and large-scale functional dynamics) that predict upcoming stimulus perception. 

Currently, at <a href='https://cbdlnbrc.weebly.com/'>Cognitive Brain Dynamics Lab (CBDL)</a>, under the supervision of <a href='https://www.nbrc.ac.in/newweb/research/groups/arpan-banerjee'>Dr. Arpan Banerjee</a> & <a href='https://dipanjanr.com/'>Dr. Dipanjan Roy</a>, I am also interested in designing mechanistic biophysical models (like neural field models) and statistical models (like Bayesian, LASSO, logistic mixed-effect regression, and Granger causality) to explain prestimulus brain dynamics in the context of sensory perception. 

Additionally, I am interested in understanding how whole-brain structural and functional connectivity dynamics change across healthy aging and neurodegenerative disorders. As part of a large-scale 3T MRI <a href='https://theprint.in/ground-reports/nbrc-dementia-research-rural-north-indians/2745675/'>study</a>, I investigate these questions in a cohort of older adults from rural North India spanning healthy aging, mild cognitive impairment (MCI), and dementia.
